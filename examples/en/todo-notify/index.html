Q01 Where should notifications appear? 決定（B）

Q01 · impact High · Can change later (needs a rebuild) · round 1

Where should notifications appear?

Decided (B)

Background: The app lets you write a due time, but nothing tells you when it is near. Most people probably have the app closed for most of the day (assumption).

Issue: Where the notification appears decides whether you notice it while the app is closed. A device notification needs a permission step (Q03) and a limit on the count (Q04).

Q03 When should the app ask for notification permission?

Why recommended: Only people who set a due time need notifications. Right after they do, the reason ("to remind you of the due time") is clear, so they are more likely to allow it (assumption).
