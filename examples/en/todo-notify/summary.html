The to-do app sends a device notification 1 hour before the due time, and keeps the noise down with a daily limit and night hours

People who set a due time get a lock-screen notification 1 hour before, and can tap "Done" from it. At most 3 individual notifications fire per day, and nothing fires at night.

Q04

How many notifications per day?

R2

After: 3 + 1 roundup

Before: 12 with no limit

3 individual a day; the rest in one evening message

Reason, accepted inconvenience, compared options
Decision (full)
Individual notifications for the 3 nearest due times each day. The rest go out as one roundup at 21:30.
Reason
Urgent ones arrive and busy days stay quiet (as recommended).
Accepted inconvenience
The 4th and later do not fire 1 hour before.
Rejected
A No limit · C 1 per day
OptionContentResult
ANotify one by one for every due item—
BIndividual notifications for the 3 nearest due times; the rest in one roundup in the eveningchosenrecommended
CSend one roundup in the morning only—
Q06

Should there be quiet hours at night?

R2

After: Moved earlier to 21:30

Before: Fires at 22:00

Quiet 22:00 to 7:00; notify earlier instead

Reason, accepted inconvenience, compared options
Decision (full)
Do not fire from 22:00 to 7:00. For due times in that window, notify at 21:30, before it goes quiet.
Reason
Quiet at night, and still in time for the due time (as recommended).
Accepted inconvenience
21:30 can be earlier than 1 hour before.
Rejected
A No quiet hours · C Leave it to the device
OptionContentResult
AFire 1 hour before, whatever the time—
BDo not fire from 22:00 to 7:00; notify about due times in that window at 21:30, before it goes quietchosenrecommended
CThe app decides nothing; rely on the device's focus mode—
